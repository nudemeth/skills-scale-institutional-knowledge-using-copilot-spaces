# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, acceptance testing, and release validation. They collaborate with developers and product managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and QA approach aligned with release plan
- Create and maintain test cases and acceptance criteria validation
- Execute manual QA and coordinate automated testing
- Identify and triage quality issues and regressions
- Participate in release readiness reviews and sign-off
- Communicate quality metrics and test coverage to the team

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Ensure reliable, high-quality releases
- Build team confidence in code quality

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA/acceptance criteria review
- Daily standups (quality status)
- Test coverage and regression reports
- Release readiness reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Review PRs for testability, provide test coverage feedback, identify edge cases
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria, communicate quality trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Report release readiness status, escalate blocking quality issues

---

## Technical Architect / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Technical Leads shape technical decisions, design system architecture, and guide engineering best practices. They partner with developers and PMs to balance technical quality with business requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review and approve major technical decisions and designs
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and promote code quality standards
- Evaluate technology choices and integration approaches
- Document technical decisions and rationale (Architecture Decision Records)

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Enable fast, reliable deployments

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and mentoring
- Technology evaluation and vendor assessments
- Risk and dependency escalation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Guide architecture decisions, conduct code reviews, mentor technical growth
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical trade-offs and feasibility
- **Project Managers**: Escalate technical risks and complex dependencies

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads define user experience, interaction patterns, and visual design. They collaborate with product managers and developers to ensure features are usable, discoverable, and aligned with design systems.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research and usability testing
- Design user interfaces, interactions, and flows
- Maintain and evolve design system and component library
- Review implementations for design fidelity and usability
- Advocate for user needs and accessibility standards
- Coordinate with developers on design specs and responsive behavior

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Reduce user friction and support burden

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and user feedback sessions
- Sprint planning (design requirements and hand-offs)
- Acceptance criteria review (UX validation)
- Design system updates and component guidelines

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide design specs, review implementation fidelity, iterate on usability feedback
- **Product Managers**: Validate user research insights, align design with strategy
- **Project Managers**: Communicate design dependencies and timeline impacts

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business requirements and technical implementation. They gather and document requirements, analyze workflows, and ensure solutions align with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Analyze workflows, processes, and pain points
- Create detailed requirement specifications and user stories
- Validate requirements against business objectives
- Facilitate communication between business stakeholders and technical teams
- Define success criteria and measurement approaches

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address actual business needs
- Minimize rework due to unclear or conflicting requirements
- Enable data-driven business decisions

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements workshops
- Requirement documentation and traceability
- User story elaboration and backlog refinement
- Impact analysis and change request evaluation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Translate business strategy into detailed requirements
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements, answer implementation questions
- **Project Managers**: Provide requirement specifications for planning and estimation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and foster continuous improvement through Agile practices. They enable team autonomy and help maintain focus on delivery cadence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove impediments and blockers to team progress
- Coach team on Agile principles and practices
- Track and report team metrics and velocity
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Escalate organizational impediments beyond team control

### Goals
- Maintain consistent sprint cadence and delivery
- Improve team velocity and predictability
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of standups and sprint events
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Team health metrics and retrospective insights
- Coaching and mentoring

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on delivery timelines and risk management
- **Developers**: Remove blockers, facilitate collaboration and knowledge sharing
- **All Roles**: Foster transparent communication and team health

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Executive Stakeholders provide business context, governance, and resource allocation. They make strategic decisions and ensure projects align with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic business objectives and success outcomes
- Allocate resources and resolve conflicts across projects
- Make or escalate strategic decisions
- Ensure project alignment with organizational goals
- Communicate business context and priorities to the team
- Provide governance and oversight

### Goals
- Maximize business value and return on investment
- Align project delivery with organizational strategy
- Reduce business risk

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and business reviews
- Strategic decision-making and trade-off conversations
- Resource allocation and priority negotiation
- Executive status reporting

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide strategic context, make governance decisions
- **Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and success metrics
- **All Roles**: Set project vision and business priorities
